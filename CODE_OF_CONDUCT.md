# Code of conduct

We want everyone to be able to participate without harassment or discrimination.
Be respectful and constructive, accept feedback graciously, and focus on what
is best for the project and its users. Harassment, personal attacks, and sharing
someone's private information without permission are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove or reject contributions and restrict participation when
necessary. To report a conduct concern privately, use the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/Hebbian-Robotics/livekit-cameras/security/advisories/new)
and identify it as a conduct report.
